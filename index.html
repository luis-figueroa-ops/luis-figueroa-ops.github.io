Sales Operations professional at the intersection of process, systems, and AI. This is where I build in public - tools that solve real problems, experiments that document what's possible when operations thinking meets modern AI.

Why I Built This

Every tool here started as a personal pain point during my own career transition after a layoff. I built micro-learning courses to sharpen skills I kept seeing in job descriptions - change management and project coordination, SQL and Power BI alongside Excel I already knew, and Python basics to hold my own in conversations where some experience was expected. Separately, I used AI to help me document years of work I had never formally written down - feeding it the history of what I actually did so it could help me articulate the business process areas I operated in: process management and improvement, change management and adoption, channel and partner operations, and budget and financial operations support - and ultimately the commercial value those contributions created. Because it is almost impossible to pull years of experience out of your head on the fly in a way that lands with a hiring manager unless you have documented it as you went. From those sessions I produced real artifacts - presentations that walk through my work story and completed Power BI dashboards. If you want to see the outcomes and the work behind them, see the Beyond the Resume section below. I also earned the Google AI Professional Certificate along the way, and its AI for App Building course, vibe coding and AI-driven debugging, is exactly how these tools went from idea to something live. These tools are the result of that same process: think-tanks designed to kick-start ideas and help you see angles you had not considered, not final answers. I share them on LinkedIn, including my featured section, because problem-solving should not stop when your employment status does.
