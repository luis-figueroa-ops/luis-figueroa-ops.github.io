How it works and your privacy
  • Nothing is saved or collected. This site has no server, database, account, or tracking. The text you paste and the files you open are never uploaded, stored, or sent to me. When you close or reload the page, it's gone. Like any website, GitHub (which hosts this page) may keep standard visit logs, but it never receives your text.
  • Your browser opens your files. Text (.txt) and Markdown (.md) files are read directly by your browser. For Markdown, symbols like # and * and link addresses are removed so they aren't read out loud.
  • Word files (.docx) use a free, open-source library called Mammoth. The first time you open a Word file, your browser downloads Mammoth's code from jsDelivr, a free public code host. Mammoth then pulls the text out of your document right on your device. Your document itself never leaves your device. A security check confirms the downloaded code hasn't been altered. Older .doc files aren't supported, so re-save them as .docx in Word first.
  • The voices come from your browser and device. Voices marked "online" in the list are provided by your browser's maker, such as Google or Microsoft, and may send the text to their servers to create the audio. To keep everything on your device, pick a voice without the "online" label.
  • The only thing remembered is your voice and speed choice, kept in your own browser so it's ready next time.
❦ ❦ ❦Voices sound different on each device and browser. Microsoft Edge usually has the most natural ones.
