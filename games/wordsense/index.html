WordSense

Your brain on words.

Build vocabulary the way reading does — through context, not memorization. Each round drops you into a real passage with one word highlighted, and you figure out what it means from the clues around it. Adaptive difficulty, four levels, and a word bank that tracks what you've earned.

Open in Claude →

Free to use, on your own Claude account. WordSense writes every passage live, so open it while signed in to claude.ai and choose "Allow" the first time it asks to use Claude.

What It Does
  • Fresh passages every round, written live by Claude, no static question bank
  • No flashcards, no definitions handed to you — the meaning has to be deduced from context, the way real reading works
  • Four difficulty levels, from direct clues to figurative use, that adapt to your streak
  • Progress, word bank, and milestone badges saved automatically to your browser
How It Works
  • Click "Open in Claude" above and sign in to your free Claude account
  • Read each passage and choose what the highlighted word means from the clues around it
  • Get three right in a row and the difficulty goes up; miss two and it steps back to reinforce the basics
