HOW TO PLAY

Sudoku: no math, just logic.

The Goal

Fill every empty square so that each row, each column, and each 3×3 box (the areas inside the thick gold lines) contains the numbers 1 through 9 exactly once. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, so you never need to guess.

How to Play

  1. Tap an empty square to select it. Its row, column, and box light up.
  2. Tap a number on the number pad to place it.
  3. Your numbers show in gold. Change one anytime by tapping a different number, or clear it with Erase.
  4. Fill the whole board correctly to win. There's no mistake limit and no game over, so take all the time you need.
  5. If every square is filled but something is off, a message lets you know so you can keep looking.

Reading the Board

  • White bold numbers are part of the puzzle and can't be changed.
  • Dark gold square: the square you've selected.
  • Lighter squares: the row, column, and box that can't repeat the selected number.
  • Select a square with a number and every matching number lights up.
  • The small number under each digit on the pad is how many are left to place. It fades out once all nine are on the board.

Tools

  • Undo: takes back your last move.
  • Erase: clears the selected square.
  • Notes: switch it ON to pencil in small possible numbers. Tap a number again to remove it. Placing a number clears it from the notes in its row, column, and box.
  • Hint: fills in the selected square, or the easiest square if none is selected. You get 3 per game.
  • New Game: starts a fresh puzzle at the chosen difficulty.

Good to Know

  • The timer pauses while this screen is open or you switch apps. Beat your best time on each difficulty.
  • Your game saves automatically. Leave anytime and pick up where you left off.
  • Heads up: your game and best times are saved in this browser on this device only. Clearing your browser's cache, history, or site data will erase your saved game and best times. A private/incognito window doesn't keep them after you close it.
